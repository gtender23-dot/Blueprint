# M21 scope — ball mechanics (2026-08-09)

Base build: cut against **`a55d2f34a2`** or later (M20 contact), per the M20
handoff. Files expected to change: `js/ui/watchphys.js`, `js/ui/app.js`,
`js/ui/sprite.js`, `style.css`, ONE recording-only stamp in `js/engine/sim.js`,
new probes in `tools/`. The engine touch follows the
`contactSlots`/`beatenDefSlot` precedent exactly — a play-object stamp no
outcome path reads.

## Source goals

From `Ref/animation goals.txt` — the "Ball ownership and flight" block:

- Ball visibly transfers from center to quarterback.
- Proper mesh-point handoffs and play-action fakes.
- Ball attached to the correct carrying arm.
- Quarterback windup, release point and follow-through synchronized to flight.
- Real spiraling pass arc rather than a moving football icon.
- Receiver hands meet the ball at the catch point.
- Deflections change the ball's trajectory.
- Fumbles bounce and tumble independently.
- Punts, kickoffs and field goals use distinct spin and flight behavior.

## What already exists (M13 / M15 / M19 / M20 baseline)

- `watchphys.js` `ballPlan` already owns a per-frame ball position: a
  `snapFlight` lerp center→QB (`SNAP_T` scaled to QB depth — under center vs
  gun already differ), QB-hold at `[QB.x, QB.y−0.8]`, mesh lerp QB→carrier
  over `HANDOFF = 0.14`, jet/pitch/direct-snap variants, pass flight with a
  lateral bow scaled by `p.qbArm`, and post-possession ride on the carrier's
  track. `ballEnd` + `constrainTrack` pin the resting spot —
  `watchphys_probe`'s outcome law (incompletions and INTs are exempt from the
  resting-spot check; run fumbles are NOT).
- `qbCue` (M13) phases the QB: snap/mesh/drop/hitch/load/follow classes;
  `throwCue` carries start/release/end + arm. `flightArc` lifts the ball
  (`wab-lift` translateY) over the flight.
- CSS ball: `wab-spin` wobble on `wp-ball-air` (±9°, not a spiral, not
  oriented to the flight), `wp-ball-loose` spin+hop, and M15's four distinct
  kick spins (`place`/`punt`/`kickoff`/`onside`) with `--kick-flight`/`height`
  arcs on the ST boards.
- `p.qbArm` (arm talent), `p.carrierSlotId`, `p.targetSlotId`,
  `p.contactSlots` (tackler/assist/ff/brokenBy), `p.covSlots`,
  `p.beatenDefSlot` are already stamped. **`p.pbuId` and `p.intPickerId`
  exist on the play result but are NOT slot-translated** — the viewer picks
  the interceptor by proximity and knows nothing about who tipped a ball.
- M20's face locks + carry poses (`wsp-pose-carry`, side-view carry arm,
  `sThrow` QB throw pose) give the sprite layer everything the ball needs to
  attach to.

## The headline defect M21 fixes

The ball is a tracked icon, not an owned object. It rides the CENTER of
whoever "has" it (visually at the sprite's feet), flies as an unoriented
wobbling ellipse, lands wherever the receiver's steering happened to leave
him (the track converges but nothing guarantees hands meet ball at the catch
frame), never deflects when a defender plays it, and fumbles wiggle on a sine
instead of bouncing. The engine already records who tipped and who picked
every pass — the viewer ignores both.

## Work items

**A. Possession rendering — ball in hands, not at feet (viewer-only)**
1. New script channel `ballCue` (labels only, like `qbCue`): snap window,
   QB-hold window, mesh window, PA-fake window, release, catch, kind.
2. New `carryCue = { id, arm, from }`: who carries after the exchange, which
   arm, from when. Arm = sideline-protection rule (ball in the arm toward the
   sideline he's attacking), seeded fallback.
3. Render layer attaches the ball to the possessing sprite's hands: QB chest
   during the hold, throwing-shoulder during `wp-qb-load` (windup), carry-arm
   offset (facing-aware, via `node._wsm.face`) for carriers, low beside the
   body once tackled-down. The ball's TRACK is untouched — attachment is
   render-only, so the outcome law and every probe read the same script.

**B. Exchanges (viewer-only)**
4. Snap: `wp-ball-snap` class during the snap window — slow tumble on gun
   snaps (flight long enough to read), nothing under center.
5. Mesh handoffs: already lerped at the real mesh point; the carry attach (A)
   picks up at `mesh + HANDOFF`, which makes the exchange read as
   belly-to-arms instead of icon-teleport.
6. Play-action fake: during the PA window the ball extends toward the faking
   back and withdraws (sine ease in `ballPlan` — script-level, deterministic,
   exempt from nothing: it ends where it started).

**C. Flight — a thrown football (viewer-only)**
7. Orientation: new `wab-aim` group in `ballMarkup`; per-frame rotation from
   the RENDERED flight direction (including the lift's vertical component) —
   nose-up on the climb, nose-over on the descent, on every pass.
8. Spiral: `wp-ball-air` gets a tight-spiral animation (fast lace/panel
   shimmer + slight roll) replacing the ±9° wobble.
9. Release/arrival blending: the rendered ball leaves the QB's hand (blend
   from hand offset over the first ~12% of flight) and lands in the
   receiver's hands (blend to the catcher's hand anchor over the last ~15%),
   killing the residual track-vs-sprite mismatch at both ends. Windup →
   release → follow-through are already phased by `qbCue`; the ball now
   visibly obeys them.

**D. The ball changes hands honestly (ONE engine stamp + viewer)**
10. **Engine (recording-only):** stamp `ballSlots = { pbu, pick }` in the
    same `plays.push` block as `contactSlots` — `p.pbuId` and
    `p.intPickerId` translated player→slot via `defBaseField.bySlot`, same
    contract comment, nothing reads it back. This is the milestone's only
    engine touch.
11. Deflection: on a stamped PBU incompletion, the credited defender is
    steered to the catch point, swats (reuses M20's `wp-tk-strip` styling via
    a new `deflectCue`), and the ball's trajectory BREAKS: seeded tumble off
    the catch point, decaying hops to the turf (`wp-ball-tipped`).
    Incompletions are exempt from the resting-spot law, so the scatter is
    legal; plays without the stamp keep today's behavior (fallback intact).
12. Interception truth: the stamped `pick` slot actor makes the pick when he
    exists in the front (proximity remains the fallback) — same wiring
    pattern as M20's tackler truth.

**E. Fumbles bounce (viewer-only)**
13. Replace the sine wiggle (run-fumble and sack-fumble blocks) with a seeded
    bounce: erratic decaying hops off the out point, tumble spin, coming to
    rest EXACTLY at `ballEnd.pt` (run fumbles are inside the outcome law —
    the resting spot cannot move).

**F. Kicks (CSS-only refinement)**
14. A real punt spirals — retune `wab-m15-punt` from a full 360° tumble to a
    spiral + nose precession. Place kicks and kickoffs keep end-over-end
    (correct), onside keeps the erratic tumble. No JS changes on the ST
    boards; `special_teams_live_probe`'s envKnown status is not disturbed.

## Engine data — tags and non-needs

- `p.qbArm`, `p.carrierSlotId`, `p.targetSlotId`, `p.contactSlots`,
  `p.covSlots` already carry everything else M21 consumes — read, not
  re-stamped.
- The ONE new stamp is item 10 (`ballSlots`). Recording-only, ~20B on pass
  plays that end in a PBU or pick, null otherwise.
- Explicitly deferred, not worked around: the sim resolves a single catch
  event — no tip-drill INTs (tip THEN pick by a second man is one credited
  event; if we ever want the chain, that is an engine extension with a full
  stat A/B). Carrying-arm switches mid-run (ball swapped away from a
  defender) stay out — one arm per possession.

## Probes (ship WITH the kit, registered in the manifest full tier)

- `ball_truth_probe.mjs` (node, deterministic — **pins Math.random**, NOT
  seedFlaky by construction; `contact_truth_probe` is the template): harvests
  real engine snaps and asserts — snap flight reaches the QB by `SNAP_END`;
  handoff continuity (ball within ε of QB until mesh, of carrier after, no
  teleport frame); PA plays keep the ball with the QB through the fake;
  stamped PBU incompletions break trajectory at the catch point and the
  credited slot actor arrives to swat; stamped picks are made by the credited
  slot actor (fallback intact when absent); run-fumble bounces end exactly at
  `ballEnd.pt` (outcome law); carryCue arm is deterministic; same play →
  same ball track twice.
- `ball_flight_live_probe.mjs` (pw): in the built game — **ball-to-hand
  alignment at the catch point is the key check** (ball transform within a
  hand radius of the catcher at catch frames); carried ball rides the
  carrier's hands band, never his feet; `wp-ball-air` frames orient the ball
  along its rendered motion; kick boards still apply the four distinct spin
  classes. Per the M15/M18 lesson, NOTHING is windowed on normalized play
  time alone — every check samples states opportunistically across frames
  with per-phase fallbacks, and phase-absence alone never fails the probe.
- `frame_budget_probe` (standing perf gate): run pre-change (baseline
  reference this container: p50 16.7ms / 31 longtasks vs recorded baseline
  PASS) and post-change; no re-baseline to green a red run.

## Exit criteria

1. Both new probes green; full core gate green; `watchphys_probe` full PASS
   (the ball still dies exactly where the engine said).
2. Ball never renders at a possessing sprite's feet — hold, carry, windup
   and down states all place it in the hands band (live-verified).
3. Stamped PBU/INT: zero mismatched identities across the probe corpus
   (fallback plays exempt and counted) — the M20 standard applied to the ball.
4. Flight frames are oriented and spiraling; release and catch blend with no
   visible pop (alignment thresholds in the live probe).
5. Frame budget: no regression vs the pre-M21 measurement in this container.
6. stat_realism unmoved (the engine touch is recording-only; the gate's band
   run proves it).
