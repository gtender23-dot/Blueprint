### B1 — designed QB runs/game: 8–12 / 5–8 / 0–1 (+sneaks; option floor 5–12)

- **Pinned by:** SR 2025 ladder (#57): elite scramblers 12–15 **gross**
  att/g (King 15.4, Brown 14.6, Mateer 12.4/14.8, Sellers 12.4/13.8, Pavia
  12.8) — subtract sacks (~1–2/g), scrambles (~2–4/g) and sneaks (~0.5–1/g)
  and designed lands ≈ **7–11/g**, squarely 8–12. ESPN (#62): 47 FBS QBs
  ≥50 designed runs (≥~4/g), verbatim. Dual: Klubnik 6.9–8.5 gross →
  designed ≈ 3–6/g, consistent with 5–8 (mild high bias in the band's
  center; within noise). Pocket: Sayin 3.0 / Nussmeier 2.6–3.2 gross at
  NEGATIVE net yards — nearly all sacks; true carries 0.5–1.5/g incl.
  sneaks. NFL cross-check (#60/#62): the league's TOP designed-run QBs are
  4–5/g and the league TOTAL is ≈1.1 designed/team-game → real pocket
  passers ≈0.
- **Recommendation: KEEP 8–12 / 5–8 / 0–1 — now source-pinned** (pick a).
  **EXCEPTION — the option floor 5–12 is LOW:** Hellums 23.4, Szarka 19.0,
  Horvath 18.7 gross att/g with minimal sack contamination. See pick (e).
- **Diff vs D6 ship:** none for the three cells (shipped 8.2 / 7.2 / 1.5 —
  all in). Option floor untouched pending (e).

### B2 — scrambles, % of dropbacks: 8–12 / 5–8 / 1–3

- **Pinned by (top + bottom):** NFL 2024 leader band 9.5–12% (#60 + the
  seeded control #64) and college leaders running above NFL equivalents
  (college y/scramble 7.8–12.7 vs NFL 7.2, #63); PFF true-scramble volume
  ~15% of NFL passing plays (#58, broader definition — sanity ceiling).
  College scrambler cell 8–12% is consistent with everything and
  contradicted by nothing. Pocket 1–3% consistent with the NFL non-mobile
  tail. **Not pinned:** a college league-average scramble rate — no public
  study exists (#63/ledger); the "college > NFL ~4%" direction stands as
  inference.
- **The dual cell is F1's home — see pick (b):** 5–8% was written for a
  faster class than the widened Dual actually is. FantasyPoints' r=.357
  (scramble freq vs pressure rate) supports rate-as-mobility-gradient, so a
  physically slower mid class at 3.5–4.5% is legitimate, not a bug.
- **Recommendation:** keep 8–12 / — / 1–3; re-spec dual to **3.5–8%**.
- **Diff vs D6 ship:** `rpo_probe` S4: the dual scramble assert `>= 1.5 &&
  <= 9` ("widened-class padding") becomes the spec'd band — **spec 3.5–8%,
  asserted `>= 3.0 && <= 9`** (the probe's standard ± noise pad on both
  edges; an unpadded 3.5 floor was tried and flaked once immediately —
  observed run values 5.1 / 3.3 / 4.1%db). Source-cited comment. Nothing
  else moves. **APPLIED 2026-08-17, rpo_probe 60/0 ×3.**

### B3 — RPO share of snaps, RPO-fit formations: 20–30 / 15–25 / 8–15

- **Diff vs D6 ship:** none (shipped 20.2 / 17.3 / 14.6, all in band).

### B4 — RPO keep share of RPO snaps: 10–15 / 5–10 / ~0

- **What the sources pin: the phase, not the number.** X&O Labs (#61)
  confirms the keep as a coached third phase (triple-RPO drills; give →
  pull → run-or-throw read chains; Empty QB-run/screen RPOs) and that RPO
  usage is explicitly QB-dependent — but **no give/keep/throw percentage
  appears in any public source found**. Only PFF Premium per-QB RPO
  charting would pin this.
- **Recommendation: UNPINNED — KEEP PROVISIONAL** (pick d). The inference
  (keep is the smallest phase, mobility-keyed, ~0 for statues) is
  qualitatively supported.
- **Diff vs D6 ship:** none (shipped 12.3 / 4.2 / 0.0 — dual's 4.2 sits in
  the padded band; same widened-class logic as F1 applies).

- **Recommendation: KEEP ~25% target — source-pinned as a 15–25% range**

### B6 — scrambles ~75% pressure-coupled (clean-pocket ~25%)

- **Pinned by:** PFF (#58), verified verbatim: "Nearly 75% of scrambles are

  charting; no college version published free. Nuance worth keeping in the
  design notes: pressure-coupling describes the SNAP, while scramble
  FREQUENCY is a stable QB trait only weakly tied to pressure rate (#60) —
  which is exactly how the engine now works (trait-scaled chance, rung
  conditioned on the pocket).
- **Recommendation: KEEP — source-pinned** (pick a).
- **Diff vs D6 ship:** none (shipped ~20% clean-pocket league share vs ~25%

### F1 — widened Dual scrambles 3.5–4.5% vs the 5–8% band

- **Verdict: the BAND was wrong for the class, not the rate.** The 5–8%
  target was inferred for a "dual" archetype presumed faster than the
  widened legLean −14..−3 population actually is. Sources support scramble
  rate as a continuous mobility gradient (#60 r=.357; #59's independent
  designed/scramble axes), so the slower half of the widened class
  legitimately dilutes the cell mean below 5%.
- **Recommendation:** pick (b) — spec 3.5–8% in the probe, retire the
  "padding" framing. No engine change; the archetype-keyed rates stand.

### F2 — pocket "designed" ≈1.4/g incl. ~0.5 broken-play/Empty floor keeps

- **Verdict: IN BAND.** Real statues (#57): ~3 gross att/g at negative net
  — after removing sacks, ~0.5–1.5 true carries/g INCLUDING sneaks and the
  occasional scramble, i.e. a real nonzero floor exists. NFL (#60/#62):
  even mobile-ish non-designed QBs log 1.8–2/g designed; the league
  average is ≈1.1/team-game. Our 0.9 true designed + ~0.5 floor/Empty
  keeps = 1.5/g sits at the top edge of plausible and inside it.
- **Recommendation:** pick (c) — accept, close the ledger note. No change.
