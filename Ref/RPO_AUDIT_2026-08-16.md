## 1 · What the engine actually distinguishes today (the vocabulary map)

This mapping is a finding on its own. Five separate mechanisms produce QB
runs, and they DON'T share a vocabulary:

**Archetypes (player.js).** Five QB archetypes — Pocket, Game-Manager,
Gunslinger, Dual, Scrambler — derived from attribute leans
(`ARCHETYPE_DERIVE.QB`): legLean = mean(SPD,AGI) − mean(STR,TEC);
`> −3` → Scrambler, `−9..−3` → Dual, else the three pocket flavors.
**The sim never reads the archetype.** Everything downstream keys on raw
attributes, so "scrambler vs pocket" in-sim is a continuous mobility lean,
not a switch. Two consequences measured below: the Dual band is nearly empty
(3.1% of starters — the −9..−3 window is too narrow), and a "Scrambler" by
relative lean averages only SPD 63 — which matters because…

**The AI dial keys on ABSOLUTE speed, not the archetype.** `setAIGameplan`
sets `qbRunPct` only when qbSPD > 75–80 (Pistol 15–25 / Spread 8–18 / other
5–12, option teams 5–12). Most derived "Scramblers" never clear those gates →
their staffs plan zero extra QB runs. The two vocabularies disagree about who
a runner is.

**Designed QB runs are mostly DICE, not plays.** In `resolveRunPlay`, ANY
handoff concept becomes a QB keep with probability
`QB_RUN_BASE[formation] + qbRunPct/100` (Pistol/RPO .30, Spread .18,
Trips .12, Single Back/Air Raid .08, Empty 1.0). This is exactly the
"any run can be a QB run" anti-pattern M3 exists to kill. Only two authored
QB-run concepts exist: **QB Sneak** (`qbSneak`) and **QB Power** (`qbCarry`).
There is no zone-read, no counter bash, no draw-family QB run with its own
blocking/routes.

**RPO is a pre-snap roll + a 2-way post-snap read — with NO keep phase.**
Run-typed snaps roll `rpoRate (dflt 40)/100 × C.RPO_FIT[formation] × 0.7 if
optionKey=qb`. Five run concepts carry `rpo:` tags (Inside Zone/Split-Zone
glance·STACKER, Power slant·STACKER, Outside Zone/Toss bubble·OVERHANG).
`rpoConflictRead` outcomes: pull/wrongPull → quick throw, give/giveLate/
wrongGive → handoff. **The QB can never keep at the RPO mesh** — note #46's
RPO+QB-run type genuinely does not exist in the engine.

**Scrambles are PRESSURE-GATED.** `qbScrambleChance(qb)` (mobility lean,
clamp 2–45%) is only rolled at three rungs: sack-escape, hurried-pocket, and
covered-sack escape. **A clean-pocket scramble cannot happen.** PFF charts
~75% of real scrambles as pressure-coupled — so the gate is 75% right, but
the missing 25% (the "nothing open, grass in front of me" take-off) is
exactly the mobile-QB signature play. Scramble-drill throw exists
(`isScrambleThrow`) but fires ~1/20 games.

**The triple-option chain is separate and healthy** (`resolveOptionPlay`:
dive/keep/pitch vs `optionKey`), fed by OPTION_CAPABLE formations (Wishbone/
Flexbone/Power-I) + SPEED_OPTION (Spread/Pistol/Trips). Its keep rates look
sane and are NOT the problem.

**Defensive counters that already exist:** `spyQB` (pulls a rusher when >3
rush, scramble ×0.55, spyEyes trait), `optionKey: "qb"` (scramble ×0.85,
RPO roll ×0.7, option crash 0.35, contain math), `edgePlay` contain/crash
(scramble ±5/−4pp, reverse/option sniffs), and the weekly-reaction brain
already switches to zone + optionKey=qb when `oppQBRun ≥ 15`. The counter
vocabulary is ready; D6 must verify it answers the new family.

## 3 · Real college reference rates

`Ref/SOURCE_LIBRARY.md` #45 (RPO 4-category taxonomy: Access / Conflict /
Triple / Hybrid) is the design framework but carries no numbers. Numeric
anchors added this session (cite: web research 2026-08-16):

- **RPO volume:** RPOs are **21.8% of all Power-Four offensive plays** vs
  8.0% in the NFL — PFF, "2026 NFL Draft: College offenses that best
  translate to the NFL" (pff.com/news/draft-2026-nfl-draft-college-offenses-
  that-best-translate-to-the-nfl).
- **RPO give/throw split:** ~**three-quarters of RPOs are handoffs**, ~25%
  throws — PFF, "Analyzing the effectiveness of RPOs over the last five NFL
  seasons" (pff.com). NFL data; college split is believed similar or
  slightly more throw-heavy.
- **Scramble rate:** NFL league average ~**4% of dropbacks** since 2018 (up
  from 1.7% in 2007); 2024 NFL leaders 7.4–12.0% (Daniels 12.0, Maye 10.8,
  Hurts 8.9) — Fantasy Points "Statistically Significant: Scrambles/QB Runs"
  + statrankings.com scramble-rate boards. College runs HIGHER than the NFL
  (younger QBs, wider hashes); PFF charts ~75% of scrambles as
  pressure-coupled ("Components of Quarterback Play: Scrambling").
- **Designed QB runs:** **47 FBS QBs logged 50+ designed runs (sneaks and
  scrambles excluded) in a season** — i.e. ≥ ~4/game for a third of FBS
  starters; a genuinely run-first QB profiles at **~7+ carries/game**, elite
  runners 10–15 (ESPN, "How running QBs changed the NFL"). Pocket college
  QBs sit near **0–1 designed runs/game** plus sneaks.
- **Ledgered:** per-archetype COLLEGE scramble-rate distributions and
  college RPO give/throw splits live behind PFF's paywall — the college
  bands above are inferred (NFL floor + "college is higher" direction).
  Flagged as a residual uncertainty for the owner; targets below carry it.

## 4 · The gap

1. **Pocket QBs run 3.6 designed keeps/game; real ones run ~0–1.** The
   formation dice floor (QB_RUN_BASE) fires regardless of who the QB is. Our
   archetype spread is 1.3× (4.6 vs 3.6); reality is 5–10×.
2. **Scramblers scramble 3.4% of dropbacks; real mobile QBs 8–12%+, and our
   league average 1.4% sits under even the NFL's 4%.** Cause is structural:
   no clean-pocket scramble rung + AI dial gated on absolute SPD>75 that
   derived Scramblers (mean SPD 63) rarely clear.
3. **RPO volume 7.1% of snaps vs 21.8% real P4.** ~⅓ of reality. The
   give/throw split (75/25) is already RIGHT — don't touch the read, raise
   the volume.
4. **No RPO keep phase and no authored QB-run family** — notes #45/#46
   confirmed at engine level: RPOs reuse run-concept art with a bolted-on
   tag, QB keeps are dice on any handoff, and only Sneak/Power are authored.
5. **The Dual archetype barely exists** (3.1% of starters) — the legLean
   window needs widening if AI call rates are keyed to archetype.
6. Option chain, sneak/power rates, and the counter vocabulary are healthy.

## 5 · Recommended design (for D6, pending ratification)

**A. The authored family (#45) — hand-authored plays with their own routes,
composed through the proven band-clamped compilePlay/grader path:**
- *Zone Read* (RPO+QB-run type): IZ give / QB keep off the backside edge
  read — the missing KEEP phase.
- *RPO Glance* and *RPO Bubble* (authored versions of today's tags, own
  routes so cards/viewer draw truth — kills the reused-run-art complaint).
- *QB Draw* and *QB Counter* (designed QB runs with real blocking), joining
  QB Sneak + QB Power as the complete authored set.

**B. The new type (#46):** a play type whose resolver reads give/keep/throw
three ways (SOURCE_LIBRARY #45's "Triple/Hybrid" categories), keep phase
riding the existing option-keep run math, throw phase the existing pull
quick-game path. Never a statistical keep on a non-QB-run play again:
**retire QB_RUN_BASE dice down to a floor ≈ 0.01–0.02** (broken-play keeps),
Empty keeps stay (no back to hand to).

**C. AI call rates keyed to ARCHETYPE (not raw SPD):** target bands, per
starter archetype —

| target (per game / share)   | scrambler | dual | pocket |
|------------------------------|-----------|------|--------|
| designed QB runs             | 8–12      | 5–8  | 0–1 (+sneaks) |
| scrambles (% of dropbacks)   | 8–12%     | 5–8% | 1–3%   |
| RPO share of snaps (RPO-fit formations) | 20–30% | 15–25% | 8–15% |
| RPO keep share of RPO snaps  | 10–15%    | 5–10%| ~0     |

Widen the Dual legLean band (≈ −14..−3) so the middle class exists; add the
clean-pocket scramble rung (roll a reduced qbScrambleChance when nothing is
open pre-checkdown — the covered-conditioned look already half-exists at the
covSack rung) while keeping ~75% of scrambles pressure-coupled per PFF.

**D. Defensive counters — verify, not invent:** spyQB, optionKey=qb,
edgePlay contain, and the oppQBRun≥15 weekly reaction all exist. D6's
`rpo_probe` must show each one bites the NEW family (keep yardage down under
contain+spy, RPO volume suppressed under optionKey=qb, wrongPull rate up
with rpoSound conflict defenders), and that `oppMem.rpo` rep-suppression
still accrues.

**E. Estimated band impact (why this is band-gated):** raising RPO volume
3× and adding keeps shifts ~8–10 snaps/game from straight runs into
RPO-priced snaps → completion% up slightly (quick game), sack rate down
slightly, scrambler-QB rush attempts ~8→~12–14/g with ypc holding ~6, pocket
QB rush attempts ~5.4→~2/g. Team rushing totals for mobile-QB teams rise;
league passing efficiency drifts up unless the pull-edge is retuned. Full
battery required: tendency + playcall + play_fidelity + compile_league +
integration + save_migration + stat_realism (or OWED-LOCAL ledger), per the
D6 block.

## 7 · RATIFIED — owner decisions, 2026-08-16 (D6 builds to THESE)

1. **Kill the dice: YES.** QB_RUN_BASE organic keeps retire to a broken-play
   floor (~1–2%); the Empty exception stays (no back to hand to).
2. **Target bands (§5C): YES, provisionally.** Owner directed a re-check of
   the compiled research from the beginning — done: swept
   `Ref/SOURCE_LIBRARY.md` + `Ref/MASTER_INDEX.md` end to end. The coach
   brain is scheme/mechanics material only (B45's RPO taxonomy is the
   framework); **no numeric rate data exists anywhere in the library** —
   this session's PFF/ESPN/FantasyPoints anchors are the first numbers in
   the project. **LEDGERED: needs brain research** — a dedicated
   stats-research pass (college per-archetype scramble rates, college RPO
   give/keep/throw splits) to firm the bands. D6 builds to the provisional
   bands; the research pass may retune them later through the band harness.
3. **AI keys on ARCHETYPE, not raw stats: YES — owner's reasoning is now a
   design law.** Three talent levels live in one game: a 63 SPD is fast in
   D3 and slow in D1, so absolute thresholds cannot mean the same thing
   across divisions. Verified this session (`createPlayer` at tiers 1/2/3,
   3,000 QBs each): archetype distribution is STABLE across every tier
   (~19% Scrambler, ~2.5% Dual at all three) while Scrambler mean SPD
   swings 64→78→94 — the label is tier-relative by construction, which is
   exactly the property the AI key needs. The current SPD>75 dial is
   confirmed broken across tiers (fires routinely at the top tier, almost
   never at the bottom). Archetypes verified working properly; the one
   defect is the narrow Dual window, fixed by decision 4.
4. **Widen the Dual band: YES** (recommend legLean ≈ −14..−3; today ~2.5%
   of generated QBs at every tier).
5. **Clean-pocket scramble: YES**, with the owner's design frame: when no
   one gets open, a MOBILE QB looks for where the extra coverage left a
   gap. Build it coverage-conditioned — the more bodies the defense drops
   into coverage, the more grass the rush vacated, the bigger the escape
   lane — mobility-scaled so pocket QBs still almost never take off.
6. **RPO keep phase: YES, absolutely.** Both surfaces: the authored Zone
   Read family carries the designed keep, AND RPO snaps gain an
   archetype-keyed keep share per §5C.
7. **Family size: ALL FIVE** — Zone Read, RPO Glance, RPO Bubble, QB Draw,
   QB Counter in v1, verified on the M1 bench.

**D6 is UNBLOCKED by ratification** (its D4 dependency still applies per
the dispatch table).
