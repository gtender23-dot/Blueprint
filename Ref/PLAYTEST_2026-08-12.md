## The five findings worth reading even if you skip the rest

**1. The 49-OVR QB report is four separate bugs, not one.**
The "especially coaching after halftime" clause is the tell. `beginSecondHalf`
(`sim.js:6325-6334`) assigns raw `school.depthChart` to the token — but the first half
did *not* use that. It used `gameDressed` → `pinnedFirst(school.depthChart, gameplan)`
(`season.js:738-770`), which is the mechanism that promotes a coach's named starter to
the front of the depth list. So the pinned QB leads for two quarters, then at the break
the pin ordering is thrown away and the depth reverts to pure `compositeRating`
descending — and the 50 takes over. The very next line refreshes `fieldAssignments`, so
the pin *table* is present while the ordering that encodes it has been discarded.
Three more compound it: offense/defense pins never write `depthOrder`, so a 1-point gap
flips on any weekly training tick; `depthChart` is rebuilt only on weeks 8/13/18 while
injuries heal daily; and `FADE` is in the picker and the resolver but missing from
`MESH_DEPTH_KEYS` — the three-places law broken in both directions (`FB` is in the sim
list but in neither table).

**2. The CB overall bug has an exact, documented cause.**
`OVR_POS_ADJ` (`constants.js:1823`) carries its own provenance comment: each value is
`(58 − that position's raw mean)`, sampled over ~70k players in Jul 2026 so every
position averages ~58. Two lines above the CB core row, another comment records that
**AWR was added to the DE and CB cores after that census** — and the lift was never
re-absorbed. AWR contributes `22 × (28 × 0.6) / 100 = +3.70 OVR` to CB and `+2.10` to DE.
CB now lands ~8.6 OVR above S and QB. The CB weight row itself is normal; this is a
stale calibration constant, not a design error.

**3. There are no conference championship games.**
`PHASES.CONFCHAMP` is day 19; `CONF_GAME_DAYS` ends at 18 and `NONCONF_GAME_DAYS` at 8,
so **day 19 is never given a game**. Conference champions are picked by regular-season
conference win pct (`season.js:1305-1321`) and the prestige and budget bonuses are
awarded on record alone. `buildAllBrackets` then fires *on day 19* (`season.js:570`) and
`schedule.js:36` renders the bracket with no phase gate — which is exactly what the owner
saw. The dashboard's own season rail already special-cases day 19 to suppress a "BYE"
chip (`dashboard.js:80-83`), i.e. the team knew the week was empty.

**4. The play-by-play "code" is escaped HTML, not a leaked concept key.**
`renderPlayByPlay` splices a `<span class="pbp-cov">` into `desc.text` at
`app.js:1430`/`1434`, then runs the whole string through `escapeHtml` at `:1451`. The tag
renders as visible text. The underlying tokens are all already prose (`"Mesh"`,
`"Cover 3"`, `"A-gap"`). The Watch-feed twin at `app.js:5442-5484` builds the identical
string into a separate variable and writes it unescaped — correct. Only the post-game
tab is broken.

**5. Dropping a lineman into coverage currently makes your pass rush better and your
coverage literally unchanged.**
The rush-3 path grafts its dropped ids onto the coverage personnel and says so in a
comment claiming the fire-zone drop obeys "the same body-conservation law"
(`sim.js:1701-1724`). It does not — fire-zone `droppedIds` reach `lbIds` (used only to
pick *more blitzers*) and never reach `defPersonnelCov`, the only thing handed to
`assignCoverage`. The dropped man's entire contribution is one scalar, `fzBonus`
(`sim.js:1728-1734`), which is then spent on `resolvePassRush` at `:1771`. Only
`droppedIds[0]` is ever read, so a second dropped man is invisible. That asymmetry —
a weighted-lottery blitz pie with per-slot shares against a 5-to-22-point number — is
precisely the "polish" the owner is feeling.

## Item detail

### Wave 1 — bugs, cosmetic leaks, and one dead surface

**5 · Highlights ordered by time — BUG, pure sort.**
`highlights.js:168` sorts by `weight` (importance) and slices 3; the box score renders
that array as-is, so the reel reads Q4 → Q1 → Q3. Records **already carry `q` and
`clock`** (`highlights.js:40-53`, off `sim.js:5246-5249`) — nothing to add at emission.
Fix: keep the weight sort for *selection*, re-sort the slice for *presentation*.
Latent defect to fix in the same hunk: `quarterOf` maps `half === 3` (overtime) to Q3/Q4,
colliding with regulation.

**5b · Scoring by quarter — render-only is viable.**
No linescore exists anywhere in the result object. But every play carries `scoreOff`,
`scoreDef`, `half`, `clock`, and `instantclassics.js:3-20` (`scoreTimeline`) is a shipping
precedent that walks `result.drives[].plays[]` to reconstruct score progression.
**Default taken:** a `linescore(result)` helper next to `scoreTimeline`, bucketed by
`quarterOf` with a real OT bucket. Works retroactively on saved games; no sim change, no
migration. Trap avoided: `clock` is reassigned to the *end-of-drive* value at
`sim.js:5703` (the code comments the hazard itself) — bucket off the scoring play's own
clock, never the drive's.

**6 · New-game gameplanning tip — copy BUG.**
The Simple-mode callout (`newgame.js:152`) talks about *start-type gating*, not game
planning, and reads as permanent. The one reassurance is generic step flavor at `:139`.
Switching later is genuinely supported (`gameplanIsSimple()` is checked at render time).
Settings already has the right sentence (`settings.js:137`). **Default taken:** reuse the
Settings sentence and append a persistent "you can switch any time — your plan carries
over" line under the toggle; keep the start-type callout but scope it to this run.

**7 · Scheme profile — safe delete.**
`renderSchemeProfile` (`gameplan.js:347-416`, one call site at `:72`) is a pure derived
view. Writes no state, and nothing in `sim.js` / `ai.js` / `coach.js` / `coachprofile.js`
reads any of its four scores. It also prints raw 0–100 values, breaching the
never-print-coefficients rule. Duplicate of `renderIdentityCard` right below it, which
says the same thing in football terms. **Default taken:** delete both hunks; fold the one
useful `identity` sentence into the top of the identity card as prose, without meters.

**11 · Cut seniors after the season — DESIGN, plus a confirmed adjacent BUG.**
Seniors stay on the roster until day 30; Cut Day is stage 4, opening day 24. `cutDayBody`
(`dashboard.js:610-656`) filters on position and class chips only, so every departing
senior gets a live Cut button — pure waste, since graduation frees the same scholarship
six days later. **The adjacent bug:** `over` is computed two different ways — display and
`atCutCap` at `:618` subtract graduating seniors, the handler at `:1872` does not. Line
`:1879` uses the raw number, so whenever the roster is over cap *before* grads and under
*after*, cuts are free and never charged against `SEASON_CUT_CAP`. **Default taken:**
exclude grads from the cut list and render them as a read-only departing strip; extract
one `rosterOver()` helper (`offseason.js:43-48` already is one) so display, cap and gate
agree.

**13 · Low-OVR starter — four bugs.** See finding 1 above.
- **13A** `beginSecondHalf` (`sim.js:6325-6334`) — route through `pinnedFirst`, or better
  export `gameDressed` so the redshirt filter travels too. **S.**
- **13B** offense/defense pins write `gameplan.fieldAssignments[...].slots` but never
  `school.depthOrder`, so `orderedIds` (`world.js:1131-1143`) sorts by `compositeRating`
  and a 1-point gap flips on any `refreshRatings` tick. **M.**
- **13C** `healInjuries` runs daily; `depthChart` rebuilds only on `INSEASON_DEV_WEEKS
  [8,13,18]`. A starter who heals in week 9 stays buried until 13. **S.**
- **13D** `FADE` in the picker (`depthchart.js:424`) and the resolver
  (`fieldassign.js:289,318`) but absent from `MESH_DEPTH_KEYS` (`sim.js:4611`); `FB` is
  in the sim list but in neither table. Derive the array from `MESH_AUTO_POOL`. **S.**

Cleared and *not* bugs: fatigue substitution (`noRotate` covers QB/K/P), in-game QB
injury benching, `buildRoleSortedDepthOrder` (worldgen + explicit button only — though
the button wipes pins with no confirm; flagged as a UX call).

**14 · PBP code in description — BUG.** See finding 4 above. Mirror the watch path: build
a local `covTag`, render it outside `escapeHtml`, escape each interpolated value
individually. **S.**

**15 · Adjustment screens reload — BUG.**
Every adjustment control is wired to `rerender()` → `renderApp()` → whole-`#app`
`innerHTML` rebuild, plus a re-run of all four listener installers. Three symptoms: focus
loss and restarted transitions; scroll restored only for the seven `SCROLL_SELECTORS`
(`.halftime-body` is listed, the timeout modal's `.to-adjust-body` and the call-sheet body
are **not**); and `restoreScroll` compensating with a forced reflow plus a double-rAF —
two extra frames of visible flicker per tap. The targeted-update pattern already exists
in the same files (the slider handlers mutate state and patch text nodes; `scheduling.js:274`
swaps a subtree). **Default taken:** ship the S tier now — add the two missing scroll
selectors, and replace chip-row `rerender()` with a local active-class toggle + tip
refresh. The general `repaint(sectionId)` refactor is a follow-up.

**17 · Conf Champ week — NEEDS-CALL.** See finding 3 above. Three options:
- **S** — truthful labelling: rename the phase to "Selection Week", phase-gate
  `schedule.js:36` so seeds aren't browsable before they're announced.
- **M** — move `buildAllBrackets` off day 19 to day 20 only (`season.js:317-318` already
  handles it), so nothing is revealed during the week.
- **L** — implement real conference title games on day 19 and have the bracket take the
  winner as champion.

**Default taken pending veto: S + M together** (rename + gate + move the bracket build).
L is a genuinely new feature that touches schedule generation, standings, SOS, seeding,
the season rail and save migration — it deserves its own pass, not a bundled fix.

**21 · Missing star on the school card — BUG.**
`scout.js:245-246` builds stars with `"★".repeat(prestige) + "☆".repeat(cap - prestige)`.
`String.repeat` truncates toward zero, and prestige is a float from season 2 onward. A D3
school (`cap 3`) at 1.6 → `repeat(1.6)` = 1 filled, `repeat(1.4)` = 1 empty: two glyphs
where three belong — exactly "1 full + 1 empty". Same defect at `newgame.js:450`. Correct
implementations already exist (`newgame.js:808`, `app.js:662`, both `Math.round`).
**Default taken:** delete both ad-hoc renderers, call the shared helper, and always derive
the empty count from the *rounded filled* value so the two halves can't disagree.

### Wave 2 — offseason flow and coaching career

**1 · Between-seasons screen.** `jobsBody()` (`dashboard.js:567-571`) is three lines that
embed `renderScheduling()` — the same component the `schedule` stage already rendered.
Because `advanceDay` fast-forwards to day 29 once the stage list completes, the player
finishes stage 10 of 10 ("Scheduling & Rivalry") and is immediately shown the identical
widget again on days 29 and 30. Its subtitle also lies ("trim the roster" — cuts were
stage 4). **Default taken:** delete the `jobs` branch and advance straight into rollover.
Secondary duplication noted for a later pass: `departures` and `cuts` both re-state the
graduating count and the whole roster.

**2 · Transfer scholarship — already implemented.** `chargeScholarship` (`portal.js:312`)
fires on every incoming transfer, and in-flight pitches reserve slots via
`portalScholarshipRoom()`. The ask is satisfied. One real leak: `chargeScholarship` clamps
at 0, so if a recruiting offer consumes the last slot after a pitch was opened,
`resolvePortal` awards the transfer **free**. **Default taken:** make it return a boolean
and have `resolvePortal` refuse, falling through to the existing quiet-reassign loop.

**12 · Force position change before camp.** Nothing special makes the transfer step
"forced" — being *in* `OFFSEASON_STAGES` is what makes a step unskippable. Position
changes today are a collapsed accordion (`convBoardOpen` defaults false) on the same
screen as the Spring Game button. **Default taken:** add a `poschange` stage between
`cuts` and `portal`, route `positionChangeBoard()` into it (self-contained, handlers
unchanged), fold `cutDayRecsBody` in so the staff pitches and the manual board share one
screen, and leave the spring board as a read-only summary. `POS_CHANGE_CAP` accounting
already spans both surfaces, so no economy change. **Watch:** adding a stage shifts every
stage index — re-check anything asserting stage counts.

**19 · Class needs vs leaving seniors — BUG.** The Search screen renders `renderGradNeeds`
(`recruiting.js:337-372`), which is purely `classYear === "SR"` counts and skips positions
with zero seniors. The correct math exists at `:813-814` (`target − returning`) but is
confined to the My Class tab. There are **four copies** of the needs formula
(`recruiting.js:813`, `:1079`, `season.js:96`, and the wrong one). `searchResults()` has
no needs filter or sort at all. **Default taken:** extract one exported `classNeeds()`
helper, delete the duplicates, swap the widget, make the chips clickable into the position
filter, and add a `need` sort key.

**20 · Contracts — three asks, all currently the opposite.**
The player coach is created with **no contract at all** (`state.js:195-230`), so
`yearsLeft` is 0, which satisfies `yearsLeft < 2` and the first deal is offered after
season 1. `getExtensionOffer` hands the UI three terms (Prove-It 2yr / Market 3yr / Max
5yr) and `acceptExtension(state, termId)` writes whichever the player clicked. **Default
taken:** seed a prove-it contract at coach creation and surface it read-only on the
preseason Week-1 expectations screen; collapse `terms` to a single AD-decided entry whose
length derives from job security, last delta and prestige; rewrite `contractBody` from
three cards to one take-it-or-leave-it card. Retention-stack accounting and the decline
path stay untouched. **Watch:** `contract_ladder_probe` (CORE) will red on any change to
`acceptExtension`'s signature — budget for updating it.

**22 · Coaching switch front and centre.** `.topbar-id` and `.sidebar-brand` have **no
handler, no `data-nav`, nothing** — both are inert decoration, so the placement is free.
But "switch teams" is two features: changing jobs is hard-gated to the offseason (and
correctly so — `acceptJob` wipes the board, scouting and retention stacks), while
switching which of *your* coaches you are (`activateSlot` / `switchTreeSlot`) is legal at
any time and is what the owner is asking for. Today that takes 4+ taps, buried in the
Coach's Office program tab — whose own comment says it is "who you can BE right now… in
the order they matter". **Default taken:** make the school name a control that opens a
sheet carrying whichever switch is legal now — tree chairs if it's a tree save, a "job
offers" row if any are pending, and nothing at all for single-career saves with no offers,
so no dead affordance. Guard: route to the agenda if `lockstepBlock` is active.
Cosmetic notes: `.topbar-nick` is ellipsised (caret goes outside the span); topbar is
`z-index 150`, drawer 200, backdrop 199 — the sheet needs 160.

**23 · New coordinator on promotion.** The slot *is* refilled — `promoteCoordinatorToHC`
(`tree.js:510-512`) rolls a fresh coordinator from the same prestige-anchored
distribution and returns `replacedBy`. Because the player gets no screen and no choice,
it reads as "the name changed". On the "Give him this program" path the source school is
*your own*, so you inherit a random coordinator on day one. **Default taken:** null the
slot and set `state.pendingCoordHire`, then gate on it using the existing staff-stage
market. Keep `staffFor` as the AI fallback and for save-load safety.

**24 · Coordinator hire window + gate.** Four paths mutate `school.staff`: the offseason
stage (intended), the Coach's Office "Replace" button (**no phase check at all — any week
of any season**), the poach "Let him walk" (leaves `null`), and the silent auto-backfill.
`advanceOffseasonStage` has exactly **one** completion gate and it's for `cuts` — so you
can walk past Coordinator Hires with a null OC and DC and play the whole year with no
coordinator bonus and no warning. **Default taken:** add the staff gate mirroring the cuts
gate; wrap the Coach's Office hire so it only fires during the staff stage; route the
poach-walk into the same market.

**25 · New job forfeits money — today it carries over.** `initBudget` folds
`coach.budget` in as carryover each rollover; `acceptJob` deliberately zeroes
`retentionStacks` but never touches `budget`, `budgetCarryover`,
`pendingScheduleGuarantee` or `pendingRetentionCost`. Notably `promoteCoordinatorToHC`
already zeroes budget and carryover — the two career-move paths disagree. **Default
taken:** zero all four in `acceptJob` next to the retention line, and notify so the loss
is legible.

**26 · Gameplan travels with the coach.** Gameplan lives on the **school**
(`world.js:948,1051,1084`), and `acceptJob` never reads or writes it — you walk into a
stranger's playbook. The only carry mechanism is a manual localStorage library on the
coach profile. `state.quickPlans` A/B/C *does* survive the move (it hangs off `state`),
which makes the inconsistency visible. **Default taken:** snapshot the coach-owned subset
(tendency, formations, fronts, situational levers) onto the new school and rebuild the
depth chart. Explicitly **not** carried: `fieldAssignments` (they hold old-roster player
ids) and `_aiScheme`. `hcMastery` already travels — it lives on the coach.

**27 · AI recruiting signed 7 DE — BUG, and 7 is a fingerprint.**
Board building *is* need-aware (`needWeight` hard-zeroes at `ROSTER_POS_MAX`). The
signing-day **backfill** is not: `fillRemainingSlots` (`recruiting.js:790-829`) sorts by
distance then vision and runs three passes, the second of which only asks "am I at max?"
— and `ROSTER_POS_MAX.DE` is **7**. Worse, `posCount` counts the whole roster including
departing seniors, so it isn't the quantity the needs UI shows. **Default taken:** count
from returning players, compute a per-position deficit, sort by deficit before distance,
skip non-deficit positions in pass 2, and hard-block `atMax` in pass 3 — leave the slot
unfilled rather than sign an eighth DE.

**8 · Default gameplans.** Two mechanisms exist, neither is a preset: `state.quickPlans`

**9 · Flushed-play ball flight — BUG ×2, one root assumption.**
`ballPlan` re-reads `A.QB.x/y` **every frame** as the flight origin, so the already-flown
segment rebases onto wherever the QB is now — a drifting passer physically drags the ball
sideways. And the bow sign `(catchPt[0] >= ox ? 1 : -1)` can **flip mid-flight** when the
QB crosses the catch point's x, snapping the arc to the other side of the throw line.
That discontinuity is the visible "wonky". Separately the flight *time* is solved from
`qbSlot.bx/by` (the pre-snap alignment) while the ball launches from the live actor, so
the M21 speed floor is computed on a distance the ball never travels and doesn't bind.
Also: `sim.js:1986` stamps `result.isScrambleThrow` and **nothing reads it** — a flushed
throw renders as an ordinary pocket dropback with no lateral component at all. **Default
taken:** latch the release point on first flight frame; solve `flight`/`tCatch`/`dur`
against the *predicted* release spot (already an expression at `watchphys.js:684`); wire
`isScrambleThrow` to a lateral roll.

**9b · INT out of the end zone — BUG in both layers, independently.**
*Sim:* the takeover spot is a flat mirror of the line of scrimmage (`100 - fieldPos`) with
no touchback branch — `touchback` exists in `sim.js` only for punts and kickoffs, never
for an interception. An offense on the opponent's 5 that throws a pick into the end zone
hands the defense the ball at its own 5. *Viewer:* the catch point falls back to a fixed
typical air distance blind to field position, and the end-line clamp that should catch it
is disabled by a `Math.min` that **widens the bound down to the bad catch point** instead
of pulling the catch point up. The interceptor is then given a hardcoded 6-unit return the
sim never awarded, starting from outside the end line. **Default taken:** stamp a real INT
spot with a touchback branch; clamp the catch point against a field-position-aware end
line; derive the return from the stamped spot; animate a kneel on an end-zone pick.
**Watch:** the sim half moves drive field position, so it churns every seeded-sim baseline.

**9c · Slow runs — BUG ×3.** The brief's hypothesis (fixed per-play duration) is wrong;
`script.dur` genuinely scales with yardage. The causes are: (i) `schedSp` is an *arrival
governor* — distance-remaining ÷ time-remaining — whose floor lets the back sag to **55%
of his rating-derived top speed**; (ii) `tEnd` pads every run with `+0.35` and derates by
`×0.92`, so a typical 11-unit inside run schedules ~76% of top speed and the `0.9` floor
pads a stuffed run further; (iii) a hidden `divM` multiplier slows **wall-clock playback
for every play in the game** by 14% in D3 and 7% in D2 — unrelated to what happened on the
play, and if the reports skew low-division this is the single biggest contributor.
Bonus defect, opposite symptom, same report: `stepAgent` clamps actor `y` to `[2, 60]`
while `endY` allows down to `-60`, so **any run over ~34 yards has an endpoint the physics
cannot reach** — the carrier pins, stalls, then `constrainTrack` slides him to the real
spot. **Default taken:** raise the ballcarrier's `schedSp` floor to ~85%, move `+0.35`
into `meshAt` where it belongs and drop the `×0.92`, widen the `y` clamp, and put `divM`
into stride/animation quality rather than the wall clock — a 0.86× time base makes the
*football* slow, which is the complaint.

**10 · Traits.** 94 traits: 11 bridges (with `desc`), 65 play, 18 flaw. **83 have no
description field at all** — the `title=` attribute is being fed `hook`, which the file's
own comment says is a developer field. A player hovering "Sticky" is told
`sepgeo man react/keep`; "Grabby" reads `PENALTY_CATALOG Pass Interference weight`. The
recruit profile omits `title=` entirely, so the two surfaces disagree. Levels render as

### Item 9 — flushed-play ball flight (two defects, one root assumption)
`ballPlan` re-read the origin from the live QB on **every frame**
(`const ox = A.QB.x, oy = A.QB.y - 0.8`), so the already-flown part of the arc
rebased onto wherever he had drifted to — a moving passer physically dragged the
ball sideways. And the bow direction is chosen by `catchPt[0] >= ox` with that
same live `ox`, so it could **flip mid-flight** the moment he crossed the catch
point's x, snapping the arc to the other side of the throw line. That
discontinuity is the "wonky".

Fixed by latching the release point on the first frame of flight. Separately, the
flight *time* was solved from `qbSlot` — the QB's **pre-snap alignment** — while
the ball actually leaves from the top of the drop, so the M21 speed floor was
computed against a distance the ball never flew. The QB geometry block was hoisted
above the solve so `flight`/`tCatch` can use a predicted release point.

**`isScrambleThrow` was dead.** `sim.js:1986` stamps it on every off-schedule
throw and nothing in the codebase read it, so a flushed QB rendered as a textbook
pocket dropback with no lateral component at all. He now escapes laterally, and
the predicted release accounts for it.

### Item 9c — slow runs (three defects, and the hypothesis in the triage was wrong)
The triage guessed a fixed per-play duration; that was wrong — `script.dur` does
scale with yardage. The real causes:

1. **The arrival governor.** `schedSp` is distance-remaining ÷ time-remaining, so
   it does not run an actor at his speed, it runs him at whatever pace lands him
   on schedule — and its floor let the ballcarrier sag to **55%** of his
   rating-derived top speed. Pacing should trim a run, not reshape it. The carrier
   now floors at 85%; every other actor keeps the old latitude.
2. **Padded travel time.** `runDist / (speed * 0.92) + 0.35` double-counted a
   ramp-up that `stepAgent`'s acceleration already models, and 0.35s of dead time
   dominated short runs. Now `runDist / speed + 0.22`.
3. **A physics clamp long runs could not reach.** `stepAgent` clamped actor `y` to
   a hard `2` while `endY` is allowed down to `-60` — so **any run over ~34 yards
   had an endpoint the physics could not reach**. The carrier pinned at the floor
   and stalled while the scheduler kept demanding speed, then `constrainTrack`
   slid the tail to the real spot: "stops dead, then teleports". Scripts now pass
   the play's true back-of-end-zone bound; anything that doesn't keeps the old 2.

**Plus the one nobody would have found by looking at the viewer.** `app.js`'s
`divM` multiplied the **wall clock** by 0.86 in D3 and 0.93 in D2 — every play in
a lower-division game, unrelated to what happened on it. That made the *football*
slow rather than making it look less crisp, and it hit new players hardest:
**Simple game planning locks you to a D3 start, so the first game anyone ever sees
played at 86% speed.** Division flavour belongs in stride and animation quality,
not the clock. Multiplier is now 1.

| 14 yd | 4.1 | 4.3 |

### What changed
1. **Only seasons that happened count.** A missing season was filled in as an even
   .500, so a program's first real year was diluted by two phantom mediocre ones.
   The window now renormalises over the seasons that exist, so year one is judged
   on year one. That alone roughly doubles a debut season's weight.
2. **`PRESTIGE_W_WIN` 0.12 → 0.40**, with a super-linear tail at each end —
   `PRESTIGE_W_SURGE` above a 70% win rate and `PRESTIGE_W_SLUMP` below 30%.
   Winning big is worth more than winning; a collapse costs more than a losing
   season; an ordinary winning year still only nudges.
3. **`PRESTIGE_BASELINE_CREEP` 0.12 → 0.05.** The baseline is what prestige decays
   toward, so a fast creep normalised a hot streak almost as quickly as it was
   earned. A run should take a few years to become who you are.

After:

```
a 1-star program goes 11-1  →  1.297            (+0.297)
three straight 11-win seasons  →  1.00 → 1.30 → 1.59 → 1.87  (displays 2★)
five undefeated seasons from 2★ →  2.00 → … → 3.83
a blue-blood going 2-14 three times →  5.00 → 4.79 → 4.58 → 4.37
a .500 program over six seasons  →  3.00 → 3.00   (unchanged, as it must be)
```

### The stability check that made this safe to ship
Tripling the responsiveness is only sound if the rule stays zero-sum in
aggregate — somebody's 11-1 is somebody else's 1-11. The probe runs a closed
12-team league for 30 seasons with win totals that sum to exactly half the
league's games, rotated so no team is favoured: **mean prestige 3.50 → 3.60,
range 1.03 – 5.78.** No inflation, nobody runs away, nobody collapses to nothing.
The division cap still holds (a D3 juggernaut pins at 3).

### The second half — measured, NOT shipped
The owner asked for two things and the other one is a worldgen change. The entire
prestige signal into roster generation is one scalar,
`prestigeBonus = (prestige - 3) * 2`, which spans just **4 points of attribute
base across a division's whole prestige range** — under half a per-attribute
standard deviation. Measured end to end:

| | bottom of range | top of range | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| D3 (1★ → 3★) | 39.9 | 43.6 | **3.7 OVR** |
| D1 (4★ → 6★) | 75.7 | 78.9 | **3.2 OVR** |

That is why a 1-star *can* go 11-1 in the first place. Widening it moves every
stat band and needs its own matched A/B, so the probe **reports** it rather than
gating it — a red light nobody has agreed to act on just gets ignored, which is
worse than an honest measurement. What IS gated: the direction must be right and
prestige must not be inert or inverted.

## Wave 3E — one chair's week is not another chair's week

**Owner:** *"camp week should inherit the recommended position changes + the
table for the changes, finalize buttons should only finalize the action for that
week. i finalized redshirts for 1 coach and went to do the other and both
coaches had been finalized."*

Two separate reports, and the second one turned out to be two separate defects
with the same root cause.

### A. Camp week now inherits the cut-day recommendations
Cut day computes a conversion board — who should move, where to, and what it
costs — and camp week had none of it, even though camp week is *when the change
actually takes*. The board's renderer was extracted into one shared
`conversionRecRows(recs, capLeft)` so the two weeks cannot drift apart, then the
recommendations, the preview panel and the remaining-cap counter were wired into
camp week above the position board.

### B. The finalize bug — one pointer doing three jobs
The symptom was redshirts, but underneath it was the same mistake in two places:
**`state.playerSchoolId` was being treated as the only program the player runs**,
when a coaching tree can have three chairs live in the same season.

1. **The preseason lived at `state.preseason` — one object for the whole save.**
   Training focus, position changes, the camp report, the spring result and the
   position review were all shared. Set the focus for one coach and you set it
   for every coach. The store is now per-school (`devCtx` / `initPreseason` /
   `preseasonSchools`); `state.preseason` is kept as a legacy mirror so old saves
   and anything still reading it keep working.
2. **`startNewSeason` handed `pendingRedshirts` only to whichever chair was
   active at rollover.** Every *other* school the same player coaches was run
   through `autoRedshirtFreshmen` like an AI program — its freshmen redshirted
   immediately, its window closed before it opened. Switch to that chair and the
   screen reads "Redshirts finalized ✓" for a decision that was never offered.
   That is exactly what the owner saw. The test is now a set,
   `coachedSchoolIds(state)` — every live chair plus whoever is active, minus
   retired chairs — and it drives both the rollover seeding and the day-5 close.

### Migration
An old save carries one global context. The chair that was active when it was
saved adopts it; every other chair starts clean rather than inheriting a
stranger's camp. Gated, because the alternative — handing coach B coach A's
half-run camp — is a worse bug than the one being fixed.

### New probe
`tools/chair_isolation_probe.mjs` — 15 checks, CORE. Verified RED against both
pre-fix behaviours before shipping: the global-context version fails 6 checks and
crashes section 5; the single-pointer `coachedSchoolIds` fails the redshirt
seeding check.

### Also fixed in passing
The item-31 `motivator` cut had duplicated the `adjustments` key in `DNA_AXES`
and `DNA_BONUS` instead of removing `motivator` — same value both times, so it
was silent at runtime and only esbuild noticed. Build is warning-free again.

### Gate note — `cutday_recs_probe` red, twice over
It failed the Wave 3E gate for two unrelated reasons, both real:

1. **It was writing to the legacy mirror.** The already-converted check set
   `st.preseason = {...}` directly. That worked when the preseason was one global
   object; now the reader consults the per-school context, so the write landed
   nowhere. Fixed by writing through `devCtx(st)` — the seam the game itself uses.
2. **The volume ceiling had become a coin flip.** "Cap-bounded trickle" asserts
   ≤4 recs per roster on unseeded random rosters, and the OVR_POS_ADJ re-derive
   (item 3) shifted the miscast rate enough that the typical draw landed *at* the
   ceiling — 20, 20, 22 across three runs. A gate check that fails one run in
   three only teaches people to re-run gates. The rosters are now pinned
   (mulberry32 / 20260812), and the number is a stable **14 against a ceiling of
   20** — real headroom, and a genuine regression detector again.

Product code was unchanged by either fix, so the gate was not re-run in full;
the probe is green 3/3 standalone. Wave 3E gate: **39 OK · 1 env-known
(`size_fit_probe`, the standing cloud fat-tail) · 17.6 min.**

## Wave 3F — item 33, the `[R]` hunk

**Owner (item 33):** *"audit gameplan screen for terminology. the players are not
real coaches and wont understand things like protection scheme options."*

The audit filed ~150 strings across three severities. This wave ships only the
`[R]` rows — the ones that aren't a taste question. CLAUDE.md help rule 3 says
*never print a coefficient, a weight table, or a threshold*, and the Game Plan
screen was breaching it in nineteen places.

Full before/after table in `Ref/GAMEPLAN_TERMINOLOGY_AUDIT.md`. The rule that
settled every ambiguous case:

> **Whose number is it?** A control the coach turns may show its own value, and a
> readout derived from his dials may show what it computes. What may not be
> printed is what the sim does with those values.

That test both convicted things the audit had missed — the depth chart printed
the featured-target threshold in two places, even though the UI already renders a
★ the moment a receiver crosses it — and acquitted two rows the audit had
flagged: the situation sheet's `AUTO — 40 / 40 / 20` is the coach's own standing
pass-depth sliders echoed back, and the `Protection Emphasis` readout is a slider
displaying its position.

### The actual fix is the probe
Rewriting nineteen strings doesn't stop the twentieth. `tools/help_rule_probe.mjs`
(CORE) scans static help copy — tip bodies plus the manual's TIPS tables — and
fails on effect sizes, weight tables and thresholds. Two design details make it
trustworthy rather than decorative:

1. **It unescapes `\uXXXX` and blanks `${…}` before scanning.** The bundler
   writes em dashes as escapes, so `— more` reads as four digits followed by a
   word (false positive) while a real `−25%` carries no minus sign at all (false
   negative). Blanking interpolations means a live readout of the coach's own
   dials is exempt *by construction*, not by a whitelist that rots.
2. **It is self-tested in both directions.** The ten strings that were live in
   the game this morning must all trip it, and six legitimate readouts must not.
   A scanner that matches nothing looks exactly like a clean codebase.

Item 33's remaining work — the `[H]`/`[M]` renames and the `tipTerm()` wrapping
pass, ~130 strings — is untouched and still open.
