## 9. SITUATIONAL / CLOCK / GAME MANAGEMENT  ⧉ sub 5  ← current pass

**Clock mechanics (what a sim must model):** `[W ✓ sportsfeelgoodstories]`
- Clock STOPS: incomplete pass, out-of-bounds (only last 2 min of 1st half / last 5 of 2nd),
  timeout, score, penalty (restarts on spot). Clock RUNS: in-bounds runs, completions in
  bounds, first downs. Play clock = 40 sec. Each team = 3 timeouts/half. Spike = counts as
  incomplete (stops clock). **10-second runoff** on offensive fouls after the 2-min warning
  (defense can decline; offense can burn a timeout to negate).

**4th-down go/kick/punt logic** `[W ✓ Malter analytics]`
- Model = **Expected Points**: go when EP(go) > EP(punt or FG). EP(go) = P(convert)×EP(1st down
  at that spot) − P(fail)×EP(opponent ball there). Inputs: conversion rate by distance (drops
  with yards-to-go, lowest goal-to-go), FG% by distance, punt net field position. Generic result
  is a go/punt/neutral verdict per (yard-line × distance) — then adjust for score, time, talent,
  weather. **This is the decision model a sim's 4th-down AI would encode.**

**Victory formation / kneel math** `[W ✓ Wikipedia Quarterback kneel]`
- Each kneel burns ~40–50 sec (40-sec play clock + snap). **Three straight kneels run off >2:00**
  (a 4th-down kneel = turnover on downs, so 3 is the practical max per series). Defensive timeouts
  extend it — each defensive TO the offense answers with another kneel. Safe-to-kneel ≈ opponent
  can't get the ball back in time to score the needed points (function of: time left, score margin,
  opponent timeouts). **Supersedes the math-less Throw Deep #47** — this has the actual numbers.
- Take-a-Knee / victory formation (Throw Deep, conceptual) — `[B47 / TD]`

**2-minute / hurry-up offense** `[W ✓ youthfootballonline]`
- 3 golden rules: (1) have a 2nd play ready — QB→coach signal costs ~5 sec, so pre-signal a
  contingency for when the ball is downed in bounds; (2) no checkdowns — throw sideline routes
  that get out of bounds, not routes tackled in bounds; (3) run what you do best (tired, stressed,
  no new concepts). Rough model: ~2–3 yards traded per 1 sec of clock.

**2-point conversion decision** (general chart logic — a specific chart source was fetch-blocked)
- Go-for-2 is driven by **score differential**: classic "go for 2" margins are when trailing/leading
  by 2, 5, 8(?)/no — the canonical spots are down 2, 5, 10, 12, 16, 18 (chase the tie/lead math);
  and modern analytics say wait until the 4th quarter to decide. ⚠ SOURCE GAP: the concrete chart
  (Football Commentary / ESPN) was blocked on fetch — seed a clean chart source before encoding.
- 2-point PLAY design (power-read/option) — `[B12]`

### Advanced-metrics definitions (full — these matter for stat_realism)
- **Expected Points Added (EPA)** — expected-points value a play adds/loses after game context.
- **EPA Per Play** — EPA averaged per play; pace-neutral efficiency. · **EPA Allowed** — EPA a defense gives up.
- **Success Rate** — how often the offense gains enough to stay on schedule (on-schedule %).
  · **Passing / Defensive / Power Success Rate**, **Success Rate Allowed** — the same, split by phase/side.
- **Explosiveness** — value gained from big/successful plays. · **Five Factors** — efficiency,
  explosiveness, field position, finishing drives, turnovers (the analytics framework).
- **Adjusted Efficiency** — efficiency adjusted for opponent, location, tempo, garbage time, game state.
- **Adjusted Yards Per Attempt (AYA)** — YPA adjusted for TDs & INTs. · **Passer Rating** · **QBR** · **PPA (CFBD)**.
- **Air Yards** — LOS→catch/target point. · **Average Depth of Target (aDOT)** — avg air yards per target.
- **Broken Tackle** — ball carrier escapes a tackle attempt (⧉ relevant to YAC/sub 4).
- **Second-Level Yards** — rush yardage beyond the DL but before the deepest level (⧉ run/YAC).
- **Standard Downs / Passing Downs** — flexible early/short-yardage vs likely-pass situations (⧉ sub 5 tendency).
- **Field Position Value**, **Garbage Time / Garbage-Time Adjustment**, **Leverage**, **Game Control**.
- **Win Probability**, **Postgame Win Expectancy** (⧉ sub 5 4th-down/late-game). · **Pace / Possessions Per Game**.
- **Interception Rate**, **Touchdown Rate** (⧉ stat_realism INT%/TD bands). · **Fumble Luck** (recovery variance).
- **Target Share**, **Route Participation** (usage) · Team ratings: **SP+, FEI, FPI, Elo, Elo Rating**.

> ⧉ These are the closest thing to the LANGUAGE of `stat_realism_harness`: success rate, EPA,
> explosiveness, INT%/TD rate, second-level yards, broken tackle. Useful as a definitional
> anchor when a probe or band is named — not a hypothesis source.
